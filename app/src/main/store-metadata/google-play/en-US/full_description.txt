Measure the noise around you — no ads, no subscriptions, no data collection.

Tisha is a pocket-size sound level meter for your smartphone. It shows the current sound level in dB(A) in real time, keeps a local history of measurements with notes, and works fully offline. No telemetry ever leaves your device.

WHAT IT DOES

• Real-time sound level meter. The dB(A) chart updates 5 times per second; min/avg/max values are collected automatically.
• A-weighting computed from the IEC 61672 mathematical formula (same approach the NIOSH Sound Level Meter app on iOS uses).
• FAST and SLOW time-weighting modes for impulsive and continuous noise.
• Measurement history. Each measurement is saved with its duration, min/avg/max, and an optional note. Sorted by date.
• Notes and titles. Describe where and why a measurement was taken — "office, HVAC running", "subway car", "kid's room at night".
• Bulk delete with confirmation dialog and 5-second Undo snackbar.
• CSV export via the system Storage Access Framework picker. Files open cleanly in Excel, Google Sheets, and LibreOffice.
• Share a measurement. A PNG snapshot of the chart plus the textual metrics, sent through any messenger or email app.
• Manual calibration: compensate your device microphone offset from ±0.0 to ±20.0 dB(A).
• Light and dark themes, Material You dynamic colors on Android 12+, font scale 100–200%.
• TalkBack and large-font friendly.
• Fully localized in English and Russian.

WHO IT IS FOR

• Tenants documenting neighbour noise, construction, or HVAC complaints.
• Office and café workers checking background noise levels.
• Teachers and parents explaining what a decibel is and what sound level is safe.
• Audio engineers and musicians estimating room noise.
• Anyone curious about how loud their flat, workplace, commute, or favourite park really is.

PRIVACY

Tisha is a no-tracking app. No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no cloud sync. The microphone is used only while a measurement is active — audio is processed in RAM and discarded immediately. No audio files are ever saved to your device or transmitted anywhere.

Measurement history and settings are stored ONLY on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all data completely.

ABOUT ACCURACY

Tisha uses your phone's built-in microphone, which is tuned for voice (300–3400 Hz). Due to physical limits of MEMS microphones and the impossibility of fully disabling system audio processing on some Android devices, readings can have a ±3–5 dB(A) margin — and a larger one above 90 dB.

The app is NOT certified to IEC 61672 (Class 1 or Class 2) and is not intended for workplace compliance, legal evidence, or regulatory enforcement. Use a professional sound level meter for those purposes.

Tisha works best as a reference tool: for comparing rooms, watching trends, teaching kids what a decibel is, and general curiosity.
