# Samsung Galaxy Store phone screenshots — capture checklist

Samsung Galaxy Store accepts up to 8 phone screenshots per locale.

Required dimensions per Samsung Galaxy Store guidelines:

- Format: PNG or JPEG.
- Minimum: 720 × 1280.
- Maximum: 1440 × 2960 (Galaxy flagships full-screen).

## Samsung-specific rules

- No mention of `Galaxy` or `Samsung` in title, icon, or screenshot text overlays (Samsung Store Terms of Service).
- Keep status-bar carrier text neutral if visible.
